# ADR 0007 — Staged Testing and Medical Review

- Date: 2026-10-11
- Status: accepted testing sequence; proposed medical-publication gate.
- Deciders: user establishes stages/resources; assistant proposes quality gate.

## Decision and proposal

User testing comes first, followed by a small group of people the user knows, then
public release. The team is the user and assistant, with quality over a deadline.

There is no qualified medical reviewer available. Propose qualified review of
published medical content as a public-release gate. Source checking and usability
testing are separate evidence.

## Consequences

Planning and later private development can proceed. Draft medical material stays
private. Reviewer access remains unresolved; disclaimers do not replace review.
No recruitment messages or public publication are authorised by this record.
