# ADR 0001 — Free Assets and Future Commercial Use

- Date: 2026-10-11
- Status: accepted sourcing direction; dataset selection pending.
- Deciders: user-established budget/business direction; assistant proposes source policy.

## Context and decision

The app starts free but may become paid. Use free anatomy sources and progressively
add detail. Prefer commercially reusable content; assess adaptation/attribution
obligations individually. Exclude noncommercial contributions from the core unless
separate rights are obtained.

BodyParts3D and HRA are candidates; Z-Anatomy requires contribution-level assessment.
No model has been imported or selected as a complete two-body solution.

## Consequences

Maintain provenance and coverage inventories. The free-assets direction does not
prove complete male/female geometry exists or remove medical-review effort.
See [Content pipeline](../CONTENT_PIPELINE.md) for sources and evidence.
