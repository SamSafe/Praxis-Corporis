## Routes

### Heart ↔ lungs (`heart-lung`)

A conceptual sequence with explicit source selections. Mesh shape and proximity do not establish flow or vessel continuity. No flow simulation or internal channel is drawn.

- `vena-cava` — **Vena cava**: Blood returns from the body toward the right atrium. Next: right-atrium.
- `right-atrium` — **Right atrium**: The right atrium receives this returning blood. Next: tricuspid.
- `tricuspid` — **Tricuspid valve**: Blood passes toward the right ventricle through this valve. Next: right-ventricle.
- `right-ventricle` — **Right ventricle**: The right ventricle pumps blood toward the lungs. Next: pulmonary-valve.
- `pulmonary-valve` — **Pulmonary valve**: This valve lies on the way to the pulmonary artery. Next: pulmonary-trunk.
- `pulmonary-trunk` — **Toward either lung**: Follow a pulmonary arterial branch toward either lung. Next: left-artery, right-artery.
- `left-artery` — **Left pulmonary artery**: The pulmonary arteries carry oxygen-poor blood toward the lungs. Next: left-exchange.
- `right-artery` — **Right pulmonary artery**: The pulmonary arteries carry oxygen-poor blood toward the lungs. Next: right-exchange.
- `left-exchange` — **Left lung: gas exchange**: Oxygen enters blood across the alveoli; carbon dioxide moves into the air spaces. Gap: Lung surface shown for context. Individual alveoli and the capillary network are not supplied by this atlas. Next: pulmonary-veins.
- `right-exchange` — **Right lung: gas exchange**: Oxygen enters blood across the alveoli; carbon dioxide moves into the air spaces. Gap: Lung surface shown for context. Individual alveoli and the capillary network are not supplied by this atlas. Next: pulmonary-veins.
- `pulmonary-veins` — **Pulmonary veins**: Oxygen-rich blood returns from the lungs through these veins. Next: left-atrium.
- `left-atrium` — **Left atrium**: Blood returning from the lungs enters the left atrium. Next: mitral.
- `mitral` — **Mitral valve**: Blood passes through this valve toward the left ventricle. Next: left-ventricle.
- `left-ventricle` — **Left ventricle**: The left ventricle pumps blood toward the body. Next: aortic-valve.
- `aortic-valve` — **Aortic valve**: This valve lies between the left ventricle and aorta. Next: aorta.
- `aorta` — **Aorta**: Blood leaves the heart through the aorta. Next: systemic-gap.
- `systemic-gap` — **Through the body and back**: Blood circulates through the body before returning to the heart. Gap: Whole-body context only. A complete systemic vessel and tissue-capillary route is not mapped here. Next: vena-cava.
